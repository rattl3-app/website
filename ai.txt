Mission:
Rattle is an AI-powered career command center designed to empower professionals facing career uncertainty. Whether you've been laid off, are considering a career change, or simply want to take control of your professional future, Rattle provides the tools and support needed to land your next opportunity faster.

Core Features:

1. Layoff Mode (Emergency Mode)
- Severance Package Scanner
- Unemployment Benefits Guide
- Emergency Budget Planner
- Assistance Resources Finder

2. Jobs Mode (Career Mode)
- AI Resume Builder
- Cover Letter Generator
- Smart Job Search
- Interview Prep Coach

3. Earn Mode (Hustle Mode)
- Gig Opportunity Finder
- Quick Cash Jobs
- Freelance Matchmaker
- Side Hustle Ideas

4. Studio Mode (Premium Features)
- Document and severance scanner
- Business starter kit
- FREE QR Codes in any plan
- AI-generated Logos
- AI Generated Stock Images
- Professional AI Headshots
- AI Product Photography
- Image Restoration
- Thumbnail generator

Available on the iOS App Store and Google Play Store.
