## Core App Modes

### 🚨 Layoff Mode (Emergency Mode)
Designed for the immediate aftermath of a career disruption.
- **Severance Package Scanner:** Understand your legal and financial standing.
- **Unemployment Benefits Guide:** Navigate state and federal assistance.
- **Emergency Budget Planner:** Manage finances during transitions.
- **Assistance Resources Finder:** Locate local and national support.

### 💼 Jobs Mode (Career Mode)
Your toolkit for landing the next big role.
- **AI Resume Builder:** Create high-impact, ATS-friendly resumes.
- **Cover Letter Generator:** Tailored letters for every application.
- **Smart Job Search:** AI-powered matching for your skill set.
- **Interview Prep Coach:** Practice with AI-driven simulations.

### 💵 Earn Mode (Hustle Mode)
Bridge the gap with alternative income streams.
- **Gig Opportunity Finder:** Real-time listings for short-term work.
- **Quick Cash Jobs:** Immediate earning opportunities.
- **Freelance Matchmaker:** Connect with clients seeking your expertise.
- **Side Hustle Ideas:** Personalized recommendations for extra income.

## 👑 Studio Mode (Premium Tools)
Everything needed to launch and grow a personal brand or business.
- **Document and severance scanner:** Advanced AI analysis of professional documents.
- **Business starter kit:** Tools for LLC formation and freelancing.
- **FREE QR Codes:** Custom branding tools included in all plans.
- **AI Design Tools:** Logos, Stock Images, and Product Photography.
- **Professional Identity:** AI Headshots and Image Restoration.
- **Content Creation:** Thumbnail generator for social media and marketing.
